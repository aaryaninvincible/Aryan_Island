How it opens

  1. Rest

    Rest the mouse on the middle of the top edge, and the panel springs out.

  2. WinAltA

    Or press the shortcut. It opens on the screen your mouse is on.

  3. Leave

    Move away and it tucks itself back in, hands the GPU back and goes quiet.

Everything,
one rail away.

Every page sits on the rail down the panel's side. Most of what Aryan Island does is a click or a right-click away from something already on screen. Here's where it all is.

  1. Home

    The song, the weather and the time, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery and mic, three sliders and live stats, all at once.

    • Right-click Bluetooth: your devices orbit your PC. Its Shared audio tab plays one sound on several outputs at once.
    • Right-click Wi-Fi for nearby networks, and join one right there.
    • Click the speaker on the volume slider for the volume mixer.
    • Click the weather for a month calendar, then the weather there for the next hours and days.

  2. Media

    Spotify, browsers, Media Player, anything that talks to Windows' media controls. Soft light swells up from below with the music, bass to treble, and the cover turns over like a card when the song changes.

    • Click the cover for the fullscreen player and its lyrics.
    • Loop and restart work even for players that don't offer them.

  3. System & RAM Booster

    CPU, GPU, RAM, disk, and top processes. Reclaim working-set memory on demand, or let the auto-cleaner run when RAM exceeds 85%.

    • Click the Memory ring gauge or Boost RAM button to flush background caches.
    • Sort top processes by CPU or RAM usage with one click.
    • Auto-cleaner triggers background trims and drops a real-time notification pill.

  4. Network

    Upload and download speed, latency and jitter over the last minute, your local and public IP, and the adapter.

    • Networks opens the Wi-Fi map: nearby networks ride the arcs of a big Wi-Fi symbol, closer the stronger they are.

  5. Widgets

    More than 80 in a searchable gallery: clocks, weather, timers, notes, stocks, US markets, and scores and fixtures from nearly 50 leagues. Small, wide or large, dragged wherever you like.

    • Add widgets at the top of the page. The page joins the rail with your first one.
    • Click any widget for more: the forecast, a stock's chart, a game's scorers and stats.

  6. Tools

    Lock, sleep, sign out, restart and shut down, the last ones press-and-hold. A timer, a stopwatch, focus sessions, a color picker, keep-awake and Do Not Disturb.

    • Right-click the tiles to choose which show and in what order.
    • A running timer counts down in a small bubble beside the pill.

  7. Settings

    The look and its colors, the edge it lives on and which screens, each picked from a small picture of the real thing. Advanced holds power, the weather city, backups and the display size.

    • The Settings tile on Tools, or the tray menu.
    • Right-click the rail itself to reorder pages, take some off, or add the Shelf and Widgets back.

The island.

Over a maximized window, Aryan Island doesn't drop the whole panel over your work. A pill shows what's playing instead, and a click opens the panel.

On the desktop, and over your apps.

With Pill on the desktop, on by default, it stays up whenever no window is under it. Pick the small pill or the long one separately for the desktop and for over your apps, each with its own size and its own set of items.

Windows notifications can arrive as pills too, and on a laptop one drops when you plug in, unplug, or run low.

Right-click either pill to arrange the long one.

Intuitive gestures.
Effortless control.

Dynamic Pond feels natural whether you use a high-precision mouse, laptop trackpad, or keyboard shortcuts.

Mouse & Bezel

Edge Strip Volume Scroll

Scroll the wheel directly on the collapsed screen-edge bezel to adjust system volume and pop the live HUD pill without opening the panel.

Trackpad & Touch

Two-Finger Horizontal Swipe

Swipe horizontally with two fingers (WM_MOUSEHWHEEL) or hold Shift + wheel scroll anywhere on the open panel to glide smoothly between pages.

Quick Click

Middle-Click Power Action

Middle-click the collapsed bezel or pill to toggle Play/Pause when music is playing, or immediately Boost RAM when idle.

Right Click

Direct System Page Jump

Right-click the collapsed screen edge strip to instantly expand the panel straight into the System & RAM Cleaner monitor.

Costs nothing
while it's closed.

One native executable in C++20, drawing with Direct2D and DirectComposition. No Electron, no embedded web view, no runtime. Frames are drawn only while something moves, and a few seconds after the panel closes the GPU is handed back to Windows.

9MBMemory while closed
~2MBA few seconds after closing
0.08%Of one CPU core, closed

Measured on a modest laptop: Intel Core i5-4210U, integrated graphics, 8 GB RAM, Windows 11, 1366×768. Private working set from Windows performance counters, as Task Manager shows it, after the app settles; closed sampled over 20 seconds. Open on Home it settles around 23 MB and 1.7% of one core; the first open of a session is about 44 MB while fonts, cover art and the forecast load.
